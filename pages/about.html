About Me

The Man Behind The Volume

"Gimme the volume, gimme the volume" — that's the sound trade mark of DJ C.O.B, THE VOLUME MASTER.

MUSIC PRODUCER · DJ

Making records at maximum volume

I'm DJ C.O.B, a music producer and DJ from Zambia. Behind the decks and in the studio, I live by one sound: "Gimme the volume, gimme the volume." Every session is about capturing energy, pushing the levels and delivering records that hit.

From vocal tracking and mixing to crafting original beats, I work closely with artists to turn rough ideas into polished, radio-ready records. Whether you need a studio session, a custom beat, a mix & master, or a full event set — the volume is always turned up.

  • 🎤 Full-service studio recording & production
  • 🎵 Original beats for lease or exclusive rights
  • 🔊 Radio-ready mixing & mastering
  • 🎙 High-energy DJ sets & event bookings
